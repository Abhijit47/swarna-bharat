## Dr. Rajini Santosh

Architect • Entrepreneur • Event Organizer • Media Professional • Social Activist • Community Leader

**_Dr. Rajini Santosh_**, also known as Santosh Kumar, is a Bengaluru-based architect, interior designer, entrepreneur, event organizer, media professional, and social activist whose journey brings together professional achievement, community leadership, humanitarian service, and public engagement.

Over the years, he has built a diverse career across architecture, interior design, event management, media, civic engagement, environmental initiatives, human rights awareness, youth development, and social service. Alongside his professional ventures, he has devoted a significant part of his life to supporting people in need and encouraging young people to participate in constructive social activities.

With experience spanning nearly two decades of community involvement, Dr. Rajini Santosh has become associated with a wide range of social and humanitarian initiatives across Bengaluru and Karnataka.

### A Multifaceted Professional Journey

Professionally, Dr. Rajini Santosh is an Architect and Interior Designer and serves as the Director of Sun Interiors. His interest in creativity, design, and entrepreneurship later expanded into the event industry.

As the Founder & CEO of Sunrisers Event Planners, he has been associated with the organization and management of more than 1,000 events and shows, including fashion events, cultural programs, corporate events, talent platforms, awareness campaigns, award ceremonies, and community programs.

His experience in organizing large numbers of events has enabled him to work with professionals, artists, entrepreneurs, young talent, social organizations, and institutions from diverse backgrounds.

For him, event management has not simply been about organizing programs; it has also provided a platform to identify talent, encourage young people, recognize achievers, and create opportunities for individuals to showcase their abilities.

### Founder of Swarna Bharat Foundation

One of the most significant parts of his journey has been the establishment of **Swarna Bharat Foundation (Reg.)**, through which he has undertaken numerous social-service initiatives.

As its Founder & CEO, **Dr. Rajini Santosh** has focused on humanitarian service, child welfare, women empowerment, environmental protection, healthcare awareness, youth development, animal welfare, and community support.

### The philosophy behind the Foundation is simple:

**“Service to humanity should go beyond words and reach people through meaningful action.”**

Through the Foundation, he and his team have worked with vulnerable communities and carried out programs intended to bring practical support to people facing difficult circumstances.

Serving the Hungry and Under privileged

### A major area of his social work has been food assistance.

Through community initiatives in different parts of Bengaluru, food has been distributed to poor families, daily-wage workers, homeless individuals, and people facing financial hardship.

During periods when many families struggled to meet even their basic needs, Dr. Rajini Santosh and his team worked at the grassroots level to provide meals and essential assistance.

He believes that even a simple meal offered at the right moment can bring dignity, comfort, and hope to someone going through hardship.

### Service During the COVID-19 Pandemic

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Dr. Rajini Santosh participated actively in awareness and relief activities.

Before public awareness about the virus became widespread, he and his team organized awareness initiatives in locations including markets, bus stations, Metro stations, and other public areas in Bengaluru.

- His initiatives during the pandemic included distributing protective materials such as:

1. Masks

2. Face shields

3. Sanitizers

4. PPE kits

5. Health and safety materials

Support was also extended to hospitals and frontline workers, including police personnel and BBMP civic workers.

During one of his awareness activities, he was involved in a two-wheeler accident that resulted in a serious injury to his right leg. His recovery required hospitalization followed by an extended period of rest and rehabilitation.

Despite that personal setback, he subsequently continued his social-service activities.

### Environmental Responsibility

Environmental conservation has been another important part of Dr. Rajini Santosh's social journey.

Through plantation campaigns connected with Bengaluru's environmental initiatives, he has participated in planting and supporting the maintenance of large numbers of saplings across the city.

His profile also records recognition connected with the Koti Vriksha environmental initiative for his involvement in plantation and conservation activities.

For Dr. Rajini Santosh, planting a tree is only the beginning; protecting and maintaining it until it grows is equally important.

### His environmental activities focus on:

Increasing Bengaluru's green cover • Protecting nature • Promoting environmental awareness • Encouraging young people to participate in conservation

#### Water for Voiceless Animals

One of the initiatives particularly close to his heart is providing drinking water to animals and birds during Bengaluru's hot summer months.

For many years, Dr. Rajini Santosh and his team have placed water bowls across different areas of Bengaluru for:

1. Birds
2. Stray dogs
3. Cows
4. Other animals

The initiative reflects his belief that compassion should extend not only to people but also to animals and the environment.

#### Supporting Children Affected by Cancer and HIV

Through Swarna Bharat Foundation, Dr. Rajini Santosh has also been involved in programs supporting children affected by serious illnesses including cancer and HIV/AIDS.

The Foundation's activities have included awareness programs, assistance to children and their families, encouragement, educational support, essential supplies, and initiatives intended to bring happiness to children undergoing difficult medical journeys.

A particularly meaningful part of this work has been efforts to help fulfil special wishes and dreams of seriously ill children.

For him, bringing even one moment of happiness to a child facing a life-threatening illness represents one of the most meaningful forms of service.

#### A Voice Against Child Labour

Dr. Rajini Santosh has also participated in campaigns concerning child labour and child rights.

His profile recounts that during a campaign against alleged child-labour practices in 2018, a legal case was filed against him and that the matter was subsequently decided in his favour.

The experience strengthened his determination to continue working on social issues despite challenges that can arise during public activism.

#### Inspired by His Mother

Dr. Rajini Santosh often credits his mother, Smt. Chithra Devi, as a major inspiration behind his commitment to social service.

A teacher by profession and someone associated with community service for many years, she played an important role in shaping his values of compassion, discipline, education, and responsibility toward society.

According to him, many of the principles guiding his public-service journey were learned first at home.
