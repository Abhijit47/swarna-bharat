## A Women Empowerment Initiative by Swarna Bharat Foundation (Reg.)

**Project Shakti** is a dedicated women empowerment initiative of Swarna Bharat Foundation (Reg.) aimed at creating awareness, building confidence, strengthening leadership, and promoting greater social and economic opportunities for women. The initiative recognizes that when women are educated, confident, financially aware, and empowered to participate in decision-making, families and communities become stronger.

Through awareness programs, leadership sessions, skill development activities, health and hygiene initiatives, legal awareness, financial literacy, and community engagement, Project Shakti strives to create a supportive environment where women can move forward with confidence, dignity, independence, and self-belief.

---

## Purpose of Project Shakti

The program focuses on empowering women with knowledge, awareness, skills, and opportunities that can help them make informed decisions and contribute more actively to their families and communities. Key objectives include:

- Promoting women's education, lifelong learning, and leadership qualities.
- Creating awareness about legal, social rights, equality, dignity, safety, and social justice.
- Driving financial literacy, economic independence, skill development, and self-employment.
- Improving vital awareness of women's health, hygiene, wellness, and mental well-being.
- Building confidence, communication skills, and encouraging active participation in community development.

---

## Major Event Activities

### 1. Women Empowerment Awareness Session

The event brings women together for interactive awareness sessions addressing issues that affect their personal, social, and professional lives, covering:

- Women's rights, equality, dignity, and personal safety.
- Decision-making and active family/community participation.
- Leadership, career opportunities, and social responsibility.

### 2. Leadership Development Program

Project Shakti encourages women to become leaders within their families, workplaces, communities, and social organizations through:

- Leadership workshops, public speaking, and communication skills.
- Team-building activities and decision-making exercises.
- Confidence-building sessions and community leadership activities.

### 3. Financial Literacy & Economic Empowerment

Financial awareness is an important part of women's independence. The initiative conducts sessions on:

- Personal financial planning, savings, and budgeting.
- Banking awareness and digital financial platforms.
- Responsible borrowing, entrepreneurship, small-business planning, and self-employment.

### 4. Skill Development & Self-Employment

The foundation encourages women to develop practical skills that can support employment, entrepreneurship, or self-employment, including awareness about:

- Vocational training, digital skills, handicrafts, and creative skills.
- Home-based businesses, service-based employment, and career development.
- Connecting participants with suitable training or community resources.

### 5. Health, Hygiene & Wellness

Women's health is an essential part of empowerment. Project Shakti promotes awareness regarding:

- Personal hygiene and menstrual hygiene management.
- Balanced nutrition, preventive healthcare, and physical wellness.
- Mental well-being and healthy lifestyle practices guided by invited healthcare experts.

### 6. Legal Rights Awareness

Project Shakti seeks to improve awareness of women's legal rights, protections, and frameworks to foster a safe, equitable, and just social environment.
