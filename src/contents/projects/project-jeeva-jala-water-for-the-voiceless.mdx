## A Compassionate Animal & Environmental Initiative by Swarna Bharat Foundation (Reg.)

**Project Jeeva Jala — Water for the Voiceless** is a flagship humanitarian and environmental initiative of Swarna Bharat Foundation (Reg.), created to provide safe drinking water to birds, stray animals, cattle, and other voiceless creatures across Bengaluru.

During the hot summer months, rising temperatures and the shortage of accessible water sources can create serious challenges for animals living on the streets and in urban surroundings. Through Project Jeeva Jala, the foundation encourages citizens and volunteers to place and maintain water bowls at suitable locations so that animals and birds can access drinking water when they need it.

---

## Purpose of Project Jeeva Jala

The primary objective of the initiative is to help provide accessible drinking water to animals and birds, particularly during periods of extreme heat. Key objectives include:

- Providing drinking water for birds and animals in urban areas to reduce dehydration.
- Encouraging compassion and promoting responsible animal welfare among citizens.
- Creating awareness about the importance of water conservation.
- Encouraging communities, institutions, and businesses to maintain active water points.

---

## Major Event Activities

### 1. Distribution & Placement of Water Bowls

Swarna Bharat Foundation facilitates the placement of water bowls at safe locations across Bengaluru. Bowls are positioned where animals can access them securely without creating hazards for pedestrians or traffic, including:

- Residential communities, streets, and neighbourhoods.
- Parks, open spaces, and areas frequented by community animals.

### 2. Water for Birds

The campaign encourages residents to keep clean water containers in accessible areas such as balconies, terraces, gardens, and safe community spaces. Citizens are urged to refill and clean these containers regularly.

### 3. Water for Stray & Community Animals

Water points are strategically established for animals like stray dogs, cattle, and other community creatures in areas they regularly pass or stay.

### 4. Summer Water Awareness Campaign

Volunteers and representatives spread crucial educational messages instructing citizens on how to:

- Keep water available and refill bowls regularly.
- Clean bowls frequently to prevent contamination.
- Ensure placement is in safe, accessible spots.

### 5. Multi-Sector Participation

- **Community & Volunteers:** Sponsoring, placing, cleaning, and monitoring local water bowls.
- **Schools & Youth:** Participating in poster-making, environmental education, and social media awareness drives.
- **Corporates & Partners:** Collaboration through corporate CSR sponsorship, logistics, and supply distribution.

## Water Bowl Care Guidelines

Placing a water bowl is only the beginning. Project Jeeva Jala highlights that maintenance is a critical responsibility, advising volunteers to:

- Check and wash bowls regularly with clean water.
- Replace damaged or contaminated containers.
- Keep the surrounding areas clear and hygienic.
