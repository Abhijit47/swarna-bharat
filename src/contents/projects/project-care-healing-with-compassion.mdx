## A Healthcare & Humanitarian Initiative by Swarna Bharat Foundation (Reg.)

**Project Care** is a humanitarian healthcare initiative of Swarna Bharat Foundation (Reg.) focused on extending medical assistance and health awareness to residents of old age homes, children in orphanages, and underprivileged families and children.

The initiative is built on a simple belief: access to basic healthcare is a fundamental part of living with dignity. Through medical camps, health awareness programs, essential medical assistance, and compassionate community outreach, the foundation works to support vulnerable sections of society.

---

## Purpose of Project Care

The program aims to bring healthcare services closer to communities that may face barriers in accessing regular medical care. Key objectives include:

- Providing accessible basic health check-ups and identifying common health concerns early.
- Supporting elderly residents with healthcare needs and promoting health/hygiene among children.
- Providing assistance with essential medicines wherever appropriate.
- Creating awareness about preventive healthcare, healthy living, and nutrition.
- Connecting beneficiaries with professional healthcare guidance when further attention is required.
- Encouraging a culture of compassion and community responsibility.

---

## Major Event Activities

### 1. Free Health Check-Up Camps

Swarna Bharat Foundation organizes health-focused outreach programs for beneficiaries at old age homes, orphanages, and underserved communities. Activities include:

- General health consultations and basic health assessments.
- Blood pressure checks and blood sugar screening.
- Weight, general wellness assessment, and basic health counselling.
- Preventive healthcare guidance.

### 2. Medical Assistance

Project Care supports beneficiaries with essential healthcare requirements based on their needs and available resources:

- Distribution of essential medicines and basic healthcare supplies.
- Direct assistance with personal medical requirements.
- Health-related information, guidance, and referral support.

### 3. Elderly Care & Wellness

Special attention is given to senior citizens living in old age homes to ensure they are not forgotten:

- Preventive healthcare, nutrition, and healthy lifestyle awareness.
- Medication-related safety and usage awareness.
- Mental and emotional well-being check-ins.
- Personal interaction, companionship, and listening to their life experiences.

### 4. Healthcare for Children

Project Care extends health and wellness support to children living in orphanages and vulnerable communities:

- General youth health assessments.
- Interactive sessions on personal hygiene and hand hygiene.
- Nutrition education and balanced diet guidance.
- Distribution of children's essential personal care and healthcare supplies.

### 5. Health & Hygiene Awareness

Educational interactive sessions are designed to be simple, practical, and easy for beneficiaries to understand, covering:

- Personal hygiene and common seasonal illnesses.
- Clean drinking water and healthy eating habits.
- The critical importance of regular health check-ups.

---

## Nutrition & Wellness Support

Good health goes beyond medical treatment. Project Care promotes awareness about the importance of balanced nutrition, hydration, physical activity, rest, and healthy lifestyle practices. Where appropriate, the foundation provides nutritional or essential food support to beneficiaries.

## Compassionate Community Outreach

Project Care combines healthcare with human connection. Foundation volunteers and representatives engage with beneficiaries through:

- Personal interaction, counselling, and emotional encouragement.
- Recreational activities to bring comfort and joy.
- Distribution of care kits, gifts, and essential items.
