## An Environmental Conservation Initiative by Swarna Bharat Foundation (Reg.)

**Project Green Bengaluru** is a dedicated environmental conservation initiative organised by Swarna Bharat Foundation (Reg.) to promote tree plantation, ecological awareness, and long-term environmental sustainability across Bengaluru. As part of the foundation's environmental activities under the Koti Vriksha Mission, and in collaboration with the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP), the initiative focuses not only on planting trees but also on their care, maintenance, protection, and long-term survival.

A greener Bengaluru means cleaner air, healthier communities, improved biodiversity, and a better environment for future generations.

---

## Purpose of Project Green Bengaluru

The initiative aims to encourage citizens, institutions, organisations, and young people to take an active role in protecting Bengaluru's natural environment. Key objectives include:

- Increasing Bengaluru's urban green cover and urban ecology through large-scale tree plantation.
- Encouraging long-term maintenance, ownership, and protection of planted saplings.
- Improving public awareness of environmental conservation, biodiversity, and sustainable lifestyles.
- Creating healthier public spaces through responsible community and institutional participation.

---

## The Koti Vriksha Mission

As part of its environmental commitment, Swarna Bharat Foundation undertakes tree-plantation activities under the **Koti Vriksha Mission**, jointly with BBMP.

The foundation's initiative includes the plantation of **more than 3 lakh saplings**, accompanied by robust efforts toward their continued maintenance and care. The emphasis remains firmly on creating trees that survive, grow, and contribute meaningfully to Bengaluru's urban environment.

---

## Major Event Activities

### 1. Tree Plantation & Sapling Distribution

- **Organised Plantation:** Saplings are planted systematically across public spaces, parks, roadside areas, and campuses in coordination with local authorities.
- **Campaign Distribution:** Saplings are provided directly to residents, schools, NGOs, and corporate groups to plant and nurture in their own communities.

### 2. Tree Adoption & Maintenance

Planting a tree is only the first step. To ensure a high survival rate, the campaign emphasizes:

- Regular watering and removing weeds around growing saplings.
- Setting up protective tree guards where appropriate to prevent physical damage.
- Constant growth monitoring and replacing failed saplings whenever necessary.

### 3. Educational & Environmental Awareness Campaigns

Interactive sessions are hosted across neighbourhoods to educate citizens on critical ecological issues:

- The long-term impact of urban forests on air pollution and climate change.
- Practical strategies for localized waste management and water conservation.
- Protecting native vegetation, pollinators, birds, and insects to preserve healthy soil and water cycles.

### 4. School, College & Corporate Participation

- **Youth Engagement:** Students act as Green Ambassadors by taking green pledges, entering creative competitions, and driving campus plantation activities.
- **Corporate CSR:** Businesses participate through funding long-term maintenance, sponsoring tree guards, and organizing collective employee volunteering drives.

## Suggested Event Program

The formal execution structure for the Tree Plantation & Environmental Awareness Drive follows this sequence:

1. Welcome, Registration, & Inauguration Ceremony
2. Introduction to the Koti Vriksha Mission & Environmental Address
3. Tree Plantation Ceremony, Sapling Distribution, & Care Demonstrations
4. Collective Green Pledge by Participants
5. Environmental Awareness Rally & Community Outreach
6. Group Photograph, Documentation, & Vote of Thanks
