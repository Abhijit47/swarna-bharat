## 💧 Water for Voiceless: A Simple Step That Saves Lives

When summer temperatures rise, access to clean drinking water becomes important for everyone — including birds, stray animals, and other creatures living around us.

While people can search for water whenever they are thirsty, animals living on streets and birds often depend on whatever safe water sources they can find.

The **Water for Voiceless** initiative by **Swarna Bharat Foundation** aims to address this simple but important need by placing drinking-water bowls in suitable locations across Bengaluru.

The initiative encourages communities to come together and provide accessible drinking water for birds and animals, particularly during hot and dry periods.

## ☀️ Why Water Matters During Summer

Hot weather can increase the need for drinking water. Birds and animals may have difficulty finding clean and accessible water sources in heavily urbanized areas.

Natural water sources can become limited, while many traditional open spaces are replaced by buildings, roads, and other infrastructure.

Providing a clean water bowl in a safe location can therefore be a simple way for individuals and communities to support animals around them.

## 🐦 Who Can Benefit?

The Water for Voiceless initiative focuses on providing drinking water for animals such as:

1. 🐦 Birds
2. 🐕 Stray dogs
3. 🐄 Cows
4. 🐈 Other animals that may visit accessible community spaces

Different animals have different needs, so water containers should be placed in locations that are safe and appropriate for the animals likely to use them.

## 💧 The Idea Behind Water for Voiceless

The concept is simple:

### Place clean drinking water where birds and animals can safely access it.

Swarna Bharat Foundation has placed thousands of water bowls across Bengaluru as part of its reported Water for Voiceless initiative.

The initiative is not only about providing water. It is also about creating awareness and encouraging people to recognize that small acts of compassion can contribute to community welfare.

### 🌱 A Small Bowl Can Make a Difference

You don't always need a large campaign to make a positive contribution.
A single water bowl, when maintained properly, can become a reliable source of drinking water for birds and animals in its surrounding area.
However, placing a bowl is only the first step.

For the initiative to be effective, the water should be:

1. Clean
2. Fresh
3. Refilled regularly
4. Kept in a safe location
5. Protected from contamination
6. Accessible to the intended animals

Regular maintenance is essential.

## 🧼 How to Maintain a Water Bowl

If you decide to place a water bowl outside your home, office, school, or community space, consider following these simple practices.

1. **Use a Suitable Container**
   Choose a sturdy container that is appropriate for the animals and birds that may use it.
   Avoid containers that can easily tip over.
2. **Keep It Clean**
   Wash the bowl regularly to reduce dirt and contamination.
3. **Refill With Fresh Water**
   Replace the water frequently, especially during hot weather.
   Do not allow the bowl to remain empty for long periods.
4. **Select a Safe Location**
   Place the bowl somewhere accessible but away from heavy traffic, unsafe areas, or locations where animals could be harmed.
5. **Provide Shade Where Possible**
   A shaded or protected location can help keep drinking water more comfortable and reduce rapid heating.
6. **Monitor the Area**
   Check the bowl regularly and clean or refill it as necessary.

   ## 🐦 Helping Birds in Urban Areas

   Urban birds may face challenges when natural water sources become less accessible.

   Small water bowls placed in suitable locations can provide birds with an additional source of drinking water.

   If placing a bowl for birds, it is important to position it where birds can approach safely and where the water can be kept clean.
