## Creating Change Through Compassion, Action & Community Service

At **Swarna Bharat Foundation (Reg.)**, we believe meaningful change begins with compassion and grows through collective action. Since 2012, we have been working across humanitarian, environmental, health, education, women empowerment, and community development initiatives.

Our programs are designed to support vulnerable communities, protect the environment, encourage responsible citizenship, and create opportunities for people to build a better future.

We believe every child deserves care, dignity, education, and the opportunity to dream.

## 05. Water for Voiceless

### A Simple Act of Water. A Meaningful Act of Compassion.

**Water for Voiceless** is an initiative focused on providing drinking water for birds and animals, particularly during hot summer months.

Water bowls are placed at suitable locations to help provide access to drinking water for:

1. Birds
2. Stray dogs
3. Cows
4. Other animals

The initiative encourages communities to take simple steps toward caring for living beings around us.
